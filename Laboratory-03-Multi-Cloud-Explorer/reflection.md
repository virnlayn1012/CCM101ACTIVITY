## Mission Reflection

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

Among AWS, Azure, and GCP, AWS impressed me the most because it offers many different cloud services that can support different business needs. I also learned that AWS can provide computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other resources in one platform.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because all three are cloud computing platforms. They provide services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and user access. They also allow businesses to increase or decrease resources depending on their needs. This makes it possible for companies to avoid depending only on physical servers.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform because every organization has different needs. A business needs to consider its budget, security requirements, workload, storage needs, performance, and expected number of users. For example, a company that expects many users may need a platform that can easily scale its resources.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing cloud services is not simply about picking the most popular platform. I need to understand the problem first and then match the business requirements with the right cloud services. I also learned how important scalability, reliability, security, and cost are when creating a cloud solution.

## 5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio became more complete and organized after this mission. I was able to document my cloud activities, compare different cloud platforms, and record the solutions I created. It also shows my understanding of cloud computing more clearly and gives me something I can use to demonstrate what I learned.
